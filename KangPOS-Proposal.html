Executive Summary

KangPOS is a cloud-based point of sale and business management platform built specifically for Australian hospitality and retail operators. Unlike off-the-shelf POS systems that bolt features onto a generic base, KangPOS was designed from the ground up around the daily reality of running a busy venue — from opening the till at 6 AM to reconciling payroll and pushing records to Xero after close.

This proposal outlines how KangPOS can replace your existing fragmented tools with a single, cohesive platform — reducing manual work, eliminating costly errors, and giving you accurate, real-time data to make better decisions every shift.

We are offering a 2-week free trial with no credit card required, full platform access, and onboarding support from our Adelaide-based team.
