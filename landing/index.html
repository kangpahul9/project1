Everything you need

One platform. Every shift covered.

Stop juggling five different tools. KangPOS handles your entire front-of-house and back-office operations from a single dashboard.

🛒

Smart Orders

Take dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders with a slick interface. Split bills, apply discounts, and process refunds without leaving the flow.

💵

Cash Management

Track every denomination in the till. Open and close business days with full denomination breakdowns, daily reconciliation, and zero-discrepancy audit trails.

👥

Staff & Payroll

Built-in payroll engine with roster integration, weekday/weekend rates, and Xero sync. Pay your team in minutes — not hours. Advance tracking and partial repayment plans included.

📅

Roster & Scheduling

Build weekly rosters, assign shifts, and clock staff in and out. Actual hours feed directly into payroll so nothing falls through the cracks.

🤖

AI Assistant

Ask anything about your business — sales trends, staff performance, cash flow gaps. KangPOS AI gives you answers in plain English, instantly.

Reports & Analytics

Real-time sales dashboards, expense breakdowns, payroll summaries, and end-of-day reports. All exportable to CSV or directly to Xero.

Xero Integration

Push payroll batches and expense records straight to Xero. No manual data entry. Your accountant will thank you.

Multi-Currency

Native AUD support with correct denomination sets, currency formatting, and payroll flows built for Australian business.
